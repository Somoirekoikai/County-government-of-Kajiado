About Kajiado County

Kajiado County is one of the counties in Kenya. It is located in the southern part of the country and borders Nairobi County and Tanzania.

The county is known for its cultural heritage, wildlife, livestock keeping and growing economic activities.

Our Vision and Mission

Vision

To promote sustainable development and improved quality of life for county residents.

Mission

To support effective service delivery, responsible development and community participation.

Leadership and Administration

County leadership and administration support planning, coordination and delivery of county services.

The county administration works with departments and other stakeholders to support development and public service delivery.

Our Values

  • Accountability
  • Transparency
  • Integrity
  • Public participation
  • Responsible service delivery
