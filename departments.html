County Departments

County departments support the delivery of services and development activities to residents.

Our Departments and Responsibilities

1. Health Services

Supports healthcare services and public health.

  • Managing county health facilities.
  • Supporting maternal and child healthcare.
  • Promoting disease prevention.

2. Agriculture

Supports farmers and agricultural development.

  • Providing farming advice.
  • Supporting livestock production.
  • Promoting food security.

3. Finance and Economic Planning

Supports county financial management.

  • Preparing county budgets.
  • Managing county finances.
  • Supporting revenue collection.

4. Roads and Public Works

Supports county infrastructure development.

  • Maintaining county roads.
  • Supporting road construction.
  • Monitoring public works.

5. Water and Environment

Supports water access and environmental protection.

  • Promoting water conservation.
  • Supporting sanitation activities.
  • Encouraging environmental protection.

6. Trade and Tourism

Promotes business and tourism development.

  • Supporting small businesses.
  • Promoting tourism activities.
  • Encouraging investment.
